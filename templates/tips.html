💡 Health & Nutrition Tips

Science-backed tips to improve your diet and lifestyle

🌅
Start with a Healthy Breakfast
Never skip breakfast. It boosts your metabolism and gives energy for the whole day. Try oats, idli, or fruit with milk.
💧
Stay Hydrated
Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. Water helps in digestion, removes toxins, and keeps skin healthy.
🥦
Eat More Vegetables
Fill half your plate with vegetables at every meal. They are rich in vitamins, minerals, and fiber with very few calories.
🍽
Eat Smaller, Frequent Meals
Instead of 2-3 large meals, eat 5-6 small meals throughout the day. This keeps your metabolism active.
🚶
Walk After Meals
A 10-15 minute walk after each meal helps digestion and controls blood sugar. Make it a daily habit!
😴
Get Enough Sleep
Sleep 7-8 hours daily. Poor sleep increases hunger hormones and leads to weight gain.
🍬
Reduce Sugar Intake
Avoid sugary drinks and processed foods. Replace with natural sweets like fruits, dates, or jaggery in moderation.
🧘
Manage Stress
High stress leads to emotional eating. Practice meditation or yoga for 10 minutes daily to stay calm.
🥜
Include Protein in Every Meal
Protein keeps you full longer. Include dal, eggs, chicken, paneer, or nuts in every meal.
📱
No Screen While Eating
Eating while watching TV leads to overeating. Eat mindfully, chew slowly and enjoy your food.
